# 🏥 MediLogs

> **Blockchain-Powered Healthcare Supply Chain**

---

## 📌 Overview

MediLogs is a blockchain-powered healthcare supply chain system designed to improve **transparency, accountability, and efficiency** in the medical supply chain. The system focuses on reducing corruption and inefficiencies in the procurement and distribution of medicines and healthcare supplies across hospitals and clinics.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

In many healthcare systems, corruption, missing stock, fake suppliers, and poor record management create major challenges that directly affect patient care. MediLogs addresses these problems through the use of **blockchain technology** and **smart contracts** to ensure that medical supplies are properly tracked from suppliers to hospitals and clinics.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

- Procurement transactions, medicine deliveries, and inventory updates are recorded on a **secure blockchain ledger**
- Once stored, data **cannot be altered or manipulated**, ensuring trust and accountability
- **Smart contracts** automate procurement approvals, transaction recording, and delivery verification
- This reduces manual errors, limits fraud, and improves operational efficiency
